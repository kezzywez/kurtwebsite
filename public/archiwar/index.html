Archiwar

A real-time strategy game I'm building in Godot for Steam. Four factions fight over the last islands of a drowning planet.

How it plays Factions Crossing the water The islands The campaign

It's set on Tidemark, a world that's nearly all ocean. The sea keeps rising, and the islands that are left are worth fighting for. It plays like the Command & Conquer games I grew up on, built around islands and the water between them.

How it plays

You build a base, dig ore, train an army and take the fight to the other side. Ore pays for machines. Cash pays for soldiers, and it comes from the land you hold, so every island you take pays you more.

Machines need a crew. A tank leaves the factory empty and waits for a soldier to climb in. Kill the crew of an enemy tank and it sits there, ready for whoever reaches it first.

Factions

Each faction plays differently, with its own units, buildings and way of climbing to its strongest weapons.

The Meridian Charter

The islands' own union, trying to bring Tidemark under one charter. Tanks, rocket troops, gunboats and aircraft. The side you play in the campaign.

The Kindled

Rebels who want to burn the old order down. They fight up close with fire, and fire doesn't hurt them. Everything they kill feeds their fame.

The Drift

A sea-going faith that lives on rafts and prays to a whale. Their Pilgrims walk across the seabed and their divers swim, so water doesn't stop them.

The Levee Syndicate

A corporation back from Earth for the land. They pump out the sea to make new ground, and their machines carry shields.

Crossing the water

Most of an army can't swim, so getting across is half the war. You can bridge a strait with timber, though a bridge also blocks your own ships, or carry troops over in boats. A few units cross on their own, and each faction does it its own way.

The islands

Every region has its own kind of map. The Charter's home waters are a chain of islands. The Drift live among open sea and scattered islets. The Kindled hold the Ashline, one great volcano between the two sides. The Syndicate's Polders are long low strips of land with channels to bridge, and High Holding is big, rocky ground.

Lava runs down a volcano in channels that shift every few minutes, and anything standing in it burns. Palm groves give the timber for bridges, and they burn too. There's a sea serpent in open water that goes for ships, and the Drift can call up a whale that throws itself onto a beach.

The campaign

You're the new commander of the Meridian Charter, a union of islands that's only a few days old. Each chapter moves to a new region and a new enemy, and starts small. By the end of a chapter the battles are much bigger than at its start.

Captain Marsh

Your second in command. He grew up on an island the sea took.

Chancellor Marrow

She talked three islands into one union, and she likes the credit for it.

Abe Rook

He runs the Free Captains. He used to work with Marrow, and he calls her a liar.

The story happens during the missions. The speaker's face comes up in a box at the bottom of the screen and they talk while the battle waits.
