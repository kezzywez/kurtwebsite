Archiwar

Archiwar is the real-time strategy game I've been building in Godot, and I'm aiming to put it on Steam. It's set on Tidemark, a planet that's almost all ocean. The water keeps rising, and the few islands left are what everyone is fighting over.

How it plays

Every building claims the ground around it. If you hold 45% of the dry land for two and a half minutes you win, even if the other side's base is still standing, so sitting back and defending isn't a safe plan.

There are two kinds of money. Alloy comes out of mines and pays for machines. Cash comes from the land you hold and pays for people. Neutral villages sit on the islands in between, and if you keep troops next to one long enough it switches to your side and starts paying you too.

Vehicles need someone inside to drive them. If a tank's crew gets killed, the tank just sits there until somebody climbs in, and that can be the other side. Sending tanks forward without infantry nearby is a good way to lose them.

You can bridge a strait to walk your army across, but the bridge also blocks your own ships from using that channel.

The islands

Some maps have a volcano on one of the middle islands. Its lava runs down the slopes in channels that shift every couple of minutes. Anything standing in it burns, palms next to it catch fire, and nothing can be built on the cone.

The larger islands rise to cliffs along parts of the coast, and a few have a river that runs across the high ground and drops into the sea.

Factions

There are four factions. The Meridian Charter is a corporate army and plays the most like a standard RTS side. The Kindled fight with fire at close range, and their commander talks like a pro wrestler. The Drift live on rafts and are strongest at sea. The Dike Guild pump out the sea to make new land, then dig in on it.

Each faction unlocks its stronger units its own way. The Kindled build up fame, the Drift build up devotion through their priests, the Guild count the land they have made, and the Charter pays for it. The campaign starts with the Charter.
